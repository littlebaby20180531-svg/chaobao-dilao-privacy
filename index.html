游戏进度与账号

《潮爆地牢》此前正式版本可离线游玩，无需注册游戏账号；即将发布的 8.0 云存档更新需要 Google 账号和网络连接，详见下节。游戏进度缓存、语言选择、已处理购买的标记等保存在您的设备中。我们不运营用于保存游戏账号或进度的自有服务器。

8.0 云存档更新的 Google 账号进度

8.0 云存档更新需要选择 Google 账号并联网游玩，每个账号一份进度，不提供本地档与云档选择。首次升级时，符合迁移条件的旧单机进度会自动关联到所选账号；已有云进度时自动接续。断网时暂停操作并自动重连；云端保存较慢时，已读取正确账号进度的玩家仍可正常游戏和充值。已产生但未同步的进度暂存于本机，并自动优先补同步充值。此前正式版本保留离线玩法。游戏读取所选账号的标识和邮箱，以区分档案和显示当前账号，并请求 Google Drive 应用数据空间的访问权限。该权限只用于本游戏数据，不用于读取您的其他云盘文件。

进度、金币、购买发放金额和防重复发货的订单标记会保存在所选账号的 Drive 应用数据空间，本机档案也按账号区分。购买时，游戏向 Google Play 提供游戏档案标识的散列值，将未完成订单关联到原档案。付款账号可能与云存档账号不同；商品发给购买时选择的游戏档案。我们不运营自有云存档服务器，不接收您的 Google 密码。

同步需要网络和有效授权。未同步的进度可能仍只在本机；请等待同步完成后再更换设备。同一账号在另一设备接续后，旧设备会暂停并要求重新登录。您可以在 Google 账号的第三方连接设置中撤销访问；撤销授权不会自动删除云档。要删除云档，可在 Google Drive 网页“设置 → 管理应用”中找到本游戏并删除隐藏应用数据，或联系下方邮箱寻求协助。清除本机数据不会删除云档，删除云档也不会清除其他设备的本机副本。删除前请停止其他设备的同步，并按需清除其副本，避免再次上传。

Google 账号与 Drive 数据仅用于账号选择、档案隔离和游戏进度同步。我们不出售这些数据，不将其用于广告或训练人工智能模型；除您明确同意的支持处理、安全调查或法律要求外，不允许人工读取。我们对通过 Google API 获取的信息的使用和转移遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括 Limited Use（有限使用）要求。云存档会保留在您的 Drive 应用数据空间，直到您删除；本机副本会保留到您清除应用数据或卸载。撤销授权会停止后续访问，但不会代替删除已有数据。

广告与隐私选择

游戏包含自愿观看的 Google AdMob 激励广告和 Google Play 游戏内购买。广告和购买功能需要联网。广告服务在根据地区要求处理隐私选择后初始化，并可能在显示广告前请求广告内容。

Google 广告 SDK 可能处理 IP 地址、设备或广告标识、广告互动以及诊断信息，用于提供广告、衡量效果和防止滥用。具体处理受您的选择、所在地规则、设备设置及 Google 的政策影响。您可以从游戏设置进入所需的隐私选项；设备系统也可能提供广告标识与隐私控制。

购买与本机存档

购买由 Google Play 处理。游戏会读取商店返回的商品、价格和购买凭据，并在本机保存已发放购买的标记，以防重复发放。我们不接收您的银行卡号或支付账户密码。

游戏通过 Unity Technologies 的 Unity IAP SDK 连接商店。发起购买、处理购买结果、确认发货或查询未完成订单时，该 SDK 会向 Unity 发送购买事件、商品与价格币种、交易标识、购买凭据及签名，以及应用版本、设备型号、系统和语言等技术信息；可用时还可能包含安装、会话或用户标识，用于其购买服务及相关服务分析。这不要求您注册游戏账号。游戏将 Unity 的广告和分析同意意向设为拒绝；此设置不会停止 Unity IAP 的上述购买事件处理，且独立于 Google 广告的隐私选择。

Unity 的服务还可能处理网络地址及其推断的大致位置、崩溃日志、诊断和其他性能信息，用于购买服务运行和分析。具体数据处理及保存规则见下方 Unity 隐私政策。

游戏币属于消耗型商品；此前未提供云存档的正式版本中，清除设备数据、卸载或更换设备可能导致进度和游戏币丢失。8.0 云存档更新会按账号同步进度和购买发放记录；请等待同步成功，并在新设备选择同一个 Google 账号。同步前清除本机数据仍可能丢失未上传进度。系统级备份是否可用取决于设备、系统和备份设置。

Game progress and accounts

Earlier public versions of 潮爆地牢 (package com.jiayou.cbdl) support offline play without a game account. The upcoming 8.0 cloud-save update needs a Google Account and a connection, as described below. Progress caches, language preferences and processed purchase markers are stored on your device. We do not operate our own account or cloud-save server.

Google Account progress in the 8.0 cloud-save update

The 8.0 cloud-save update requires a Google Account and a network connection, with one progress record per account and no local/cloud save picker. Eligible older offline progress is linked automatically on first upgrade; existing cloud progress resumes automatically. When disconnected, play pauses and reconnects automatically. Once the correct account progress is loaded, slow cloud saving does not block play or purchases. Unsynced progress is cached on the device, with credited purchases given priority for cloud saving. Earlier public versions retain their offline behavior. The game reads the selected account identifier and email address to separate profiles and show the active account, and requests access to its own application data space in Google Drive. This access is for this game's data, not your other Drive files.

Progress, coins, purchase grant amounts and order markers used to prevent duplicate delivery are stored in the selected account's Drive application data space. Local profiles are also kept separately by account. When you buy, the game sends Google Play a hash of the game profile identifier to route unfinished orders to their original profile. Your payment account may differ from your cloud-save account; items go to the game profile selected when the purchase starts. We do not operate our own cloud-save server or receive your Google password.

Sync needs a network connection and valid authorization. Unsynced progress may exist only on this device; wait for sync to finish before changing devices. If the account continues on another device, the previous device pauses and asks you to sign in again. You can revoke access in your Google Account's third-party connections; doing so does not automatically delete existing cloud saves. To delete cloud saves, find this game under Settings → Manage apps in Drive on the web and delete its hidden app data, or contact support below for help. Clearing local data does not delete cloud saves, and deleting cloud saves does not erase copies on other devices. Stop syncing on those devices and clear their copies as needed before deletion to prevent another upload.

Google Account and Drive data are used only for account selection, profile separation and game-progress sync. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or train AI models with it. We do not allow human access except with your explicit consent for support, for security investigations, or as required by law. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Cloud saves remain in your Drive application data space until you delete them. Local copies remain until you clear app data or uninstall. Revoking authorization stops future access but does not delete data already stored.

Advertising and privacy choices

The game includes optional Google AdMob rewarded ads and Google Play in-app purchases, which require an internet connection. Advertising services initialize after applicable privacy choices are handled and may request ad content before you choose to watch a video.

Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Processing depends on applicable rules, your choices, device settings and Google's policies. Required privacy options are available from the game settings; your device may also offer advertising privacy controls.

Purchases and local saves

Google Play processes purchases. The game reads product information, store prices and purchase receipts, and stores a local record of completed grants to prevent duplicates. We do not receive your card number or payment-account password.

The game connects to the store through Unity Technologies' Unity IAP SDK. When a purchase is attempted, its result is processed, delivery is confirmed, or pending orders are fetched, the SDK sends Unity purchase events, product and price/currency information, transaction identifiers, purchase receipts and signatures, and technical information such as app version, device model, operating system and language. Installation, session or user identifiers may also be included when available, for Unity's purchasing services and related service analytics. This does not require a game account. The game sets Unity's advertising and analytics consent intents to denied. This setting does not stop the IAP purchase-event processing described above and is separate from Google advertising privacy choices.

Unity services may also process network addresses and the approximate location inferred from them, crash logs, diagnostics and other performance information for purchasing-service operation and analytics. See Unity's privacy policy below for its data handling and retention practices.

Coins are consumable items. In earlier public versions without cloud saves, clearing app data, uninstalling or changing devices can remove progress and currency. The 8.0 cloud-save update syncs progress and purchase grants by account. Wait for sync to finish and choose the same Google Account on the new device. Clearing local data before sync can still lose unsynced progress. Any system backup depends on your device, operating system and backup settings.

Deleting data and contacting us

If you choose to contact us by email, we receive your email address, message and any device model, operating system or game version information you keep in the message, to respond to your feedback. Your email app shows the message before sending; you can edit it or choose not to send it. You may request deletion of related messages at the same address, except where retention is legally required.

You can erase local data through your device's app settings. This removes device caches, unsynced progress or older offline progress, but does not delete cloud saves; see the cloud deletion instructions above. Contact game support for assistance with access or deletion requests concerning Unity IAP data; handling and necessary retention follow applicable rules.
