Fantasy dungeon adventure

Explore dungeon floors, collect relics and build your warriors' base. Earlier public versions support offline play; the new account-based internal test requires a connection. Optional rewarded ads and coin purchases use Google services.

Google Account progress in the new internal test

The new internal test uses one progress record per Google Account. Choose an account to continue across devices. It requires a connection: if disconnected, play pauses and reconnects automatically. Slow cloud uploads run in the background without blocking play or purchases. Players do not choose between local and cloud saves. Earlier public versions retain their offline behavior.

With your permission, the game uses your account identifier and email to separate profiles. Google Drive access is limited to this game's hidden application data: progress, coins and purchase-delivery markers. It does not read your other Drive files. The developer does not operate a cloud-save server.

Read the Privacy Policy for data use, retention and deletion instructions. You do not need to sign in to read this website.
